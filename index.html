Scroll

A single continuous gradient that shifts beneath the page as you move through it. No JavaScript, no images — just a scroll-driven timeline.

Scroll
01

Dawn

The backdrop is one fixed layer behind everything. As the document scrolls, its hue rotates and its stops drift — so the color you see is a function of scroll position rather than of any single section.

02

Drift

Each panel fades and lifts into place on its own timeline, driven by animation-timeline: view(). The browser handles the easing against the scroller; nothing polls a scroll event.

03

Depth

A soft grain layer sits on top of the gradient at low opacity. It breaks up the banding that large, slow color transitions tend to show on 8-bit displays.

04

Dusk

By the bottom of the page the gradient has travelled the full sweep of its palette. Scroll back up and it retraces the same path exactly — the animation is bound to position, not to time.

Scroll timeline

The backdrop's progress maps 1:1 to how far down the document you are.

View timeline

Panels animate relative to their own intersection with the viewport.

Graceful fallback

Where timelines aren't supported the gradient stays put and content simply shows.

Built with two files and no dependencies.
